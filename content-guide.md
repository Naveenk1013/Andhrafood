# content-guide.md: Taxonomy, Style and Seed List

## 1. Taxonomy

**Categories**
Tiffins (breakfast) · Rice and meals · Pappu and dals · Curries and kuras · Fries and vepudu · Pulusu and charu · Pickles and chutneys · Podis · Snacks and street food · Sweets · Seafood · Meat and poultry · Festival dishes · Tribal and forest foods · Beverages · Dairy and curd preparations

**Regions**
- **Uttarandhra:** northern coastal and tribal belt, including Araku
- **Coastal Andhra:** Godavari and Krishna-Guntur deltas, rice, seafood, sweets
- **Rayalaseema:** drier south-west, millets (ragi, jowar), robust non-veg, bold spice

**Diet labels:** vegetarian, non-vegetarian, eggetarian, vegan-adaptable
**Heat scale:** 1 to 5 (see `design.md`)
**Occasions:** Ugadi, Sankranti, Vinayaka Chaviti, Dasara, Deepavali, weddings, everyday meals, fasting days, monsoon seasonal foods

## 2. Dish entry quality bar
1. Telugu name, transliteration, English name
2. One-paragraph story: what it is, where it is from, when it is eaten (original wording)
3. Ingredients with metric plus household measures
4. Numbered method with times and sensory cues ("until the edges lift")
5. At least one tip and one common mistake
6. What it is served with
7. Variations by region or household
8. Sources (minimum one; two for any historical or GI claim)
9. Allergens and diet label
10. Status and reviewer

## 3. Writing style
- Clear, warm, factual. Short sentences. No hype words ("amazing", "mouth-watering").
- Explain the why of a technique in one line.
- Use Telugu terms in transliteration with a gloss the first time: *popu* (tempering).
- Be honest about uncertainty: "Often said to originate in..." only if sourced.
- Do not state that a dish is "authentic" or "the only right way". Describe variation.
- Inclusive of communities and households; avoid claims about caste or community unless carefully sourced and relevant.

## 4. Fact categories and evidence needed
| Claim type | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Recipe quantities and method | Tested or cross-checked against 2+ reliable sources or a home cook reviewer |
| Origin or history | 2+ sources, hedged wording if unclear |
| GI tag | Official GI registry listing |
| Festival association | Source or reviewer |
| Nutrition or health claims | Avoid in MVP |

## 5. Seed dish candidates
**All items below are candidates from general knowledge and must be researched and verified before publishing.** Regional placement and origin claims especially need confirmation.

**Tiffins:** Pesarattu with upma, Idli, Dosa varieties, Attu, Punugulu, Garelu, Ghee karam dosa, Uggani (Rayalaseema puffed rice dish), Mirchi bajji
**Rice and meals:** Pulihora, Daddojanam (curd rice), Natu kodi pulao variants, Andhra-style biryani (Andhra version only), Annam with pappu and ghee, Ragi sangati with curry
**Pappu and charu:** Mudda pappu, Dosakaya pappu, Tomato pappu, Pappu charu, Ulava charu (horse gram), Mamidikaya pappu
**Curries and fries:** Gutti vankaya kura, Bendakaya vepudu, Dondakaya fry, Aratikaya (raw banana) fry, Gongura mamsam, Natu kodi pulusu, Kodi vepudu, Mutton fry (Rayalaseema style)
**Seafood:** Chepala pulusu, Royyala iguru, Pulasa pulusu (seasonal, Godavari), Vanjaram fry, Crab masala (Andhra style)
**Pickles and chutneys:** Avakaya, Gongura pachadi, Allam pachadi, Tomato pachadi, Magaya, Pachi mirchi pickle
**Podis:** Kandi podi, Karam podi (idli podi), Nalla karam
**Sweets:** Bobbatlu, Ariselu, Pootharekulu (Atreyapuram), Bandar laddu (Machilipatnam), Kakinada kaja, Sunnundalu, Mamidi tandra (mango leather), Tirupati laddu (temple prasadam, handle with care and sources)
**Tribal and forest:** Bamboo chicken (Araku), Bamboo shoot preparations, Millet gruel
**Beverages:** Araku coffee, Buttermilk (majjiga) with seasoning, Panakam (festival drink)

**Ingredients to cover:** Guntur sannam chilli, gongura, tamarind, curry leaves, horse gram, ragi, green gram, raw mango, sesame oil, jaggery, tindora, ridge gourd, drumstick, country chicken, Godavari fish and prawns, coconut, ghee.

**Techniques:** popu (tempering), rolu and rubbing stone grinding, pickling and sun-drying (avakaya season), sangati making, bamboo cooking, clay pot cooking, dum, vadiyalu and appadalu sun-drying, jaggery syrup stages.

## 6. Review workflow
`draft` (agent or writer) → `needs-review` (self-check, sources attached) → `verified` (human reviewer with name and date) → `published`.
Anything the AI drafts stays `draft` until a human checks it against sources.
