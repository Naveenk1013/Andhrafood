# roadmap.md: Beyond the MVP

## Phase 1: MVP beta (see mvp.md)
60 to 80 dishes, region map, search, Ruchi, PWA, English and Telugu.

## Phase 2: Depth and community
- Expand to 200+ dishes, all 26 districts represented
- Full Telugu content for all entries
- Seasonal calendar ("what Andhra eats in each month")
- Cookware and kitchen heritage section
- Contributor programme: submit family recipes for review
- Newsletter and share cards

## Phase 3: Smarter kitchen
- Voice questions and spoken cooking guidance in Telugu
- "Cook with what I have" mode
- Meal planner and shopping list (traditional full meals, festival menus)
- Optional accounts with synced favourites

## Phase 4: Discovery and culture
- Food trail maps by district (food only)
- Eatery and sweet-shop directory with verified listings
- Short video and audio of cooks and techniques
- Photo-based dish identification

## Phase 5: Platform
- Public read API for educators and researchers
- Teaching pack for hospitality courses
- Native app wrappers if usage justifies
- Partnerships with chefs, institutions and local producers

## Guardrail for all phases
Stay focused on Andhra Pradesh food and culinary practice. Quality and trust come before breadth.
