## Overview
Coastal Andhra extends along the Bay of Bengal, cradled by the fertile alluvial deltas of the Krishna and Godavari rivers. Known as the rice bowl of Andhra Pradesh, its food balances pungent heat with sharp sourness from raw mango, tamarind, and sorrel leaves (gongura).
