## Overview
Popu (or talimpu) is the soul of Andhra seasoning. Whole spices—mustard seeds (aavalu), cumin seeds (jeelakarra), split urad dal (minapappu), chana dal (senagapappu), dried red chillies, asafoetida (hing), and fresh curry leaves (karivepaku)—are bloomed in hot oil or ghee, awakening their volatile aromatics before folding into curries, dals, and chutneys.
