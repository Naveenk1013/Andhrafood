## Overview
Rolu rokkali grinding is the age-old method of stone-crushing roasted lentils, whole spices, herbs, and pickles. Unlike high-speed electric blenders that generate heat and aerate the ingredients, stone pounding fractures oil cells slowly, producing the coarse, intensely fragrant texture characteristic of authentic Andhra pachadis.
